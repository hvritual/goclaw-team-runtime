---
title: Projects
description: Organize related issues and tasks around a shared outcome.
---

Projects group related work around a goal. A project can have a title, description, icon, member lead, status, priority, start date, and due date.

## Progress

Project progress is derived from its linked issues. Completing or cancelling an issue contributes to the completed count; open issues remain in the total.

## Leads

A project lead is a workspace member who is accountable for coordination. The lead is a planning signal, not a separate permission level.

Deleting a project clears the project reference from its issues and tasks while preserving those records.
