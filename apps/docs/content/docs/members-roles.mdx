---
title: Members and roles
description: Invite people and manage workspace access from one settings area.
---

Multica has three workspace roles:

- **Owner** — full control, including workspace deletion and ownership management.
- **Admin** — manages workspace configuration, members, roles, projects, and shared definitions.
- **Member** — collaborates on projects, issues, tasks, comments, and skills.

## Manage members

Owners and administrators can invite people, change member roles, and remove members from **Settings → Members**. Invitations remain visible until accepted or revoked.

## Review permissions

The permission explanation is available under **Settings → Roles** to owners and administrators. Ordinary members do not see this tab or its permission catalog; their everyday workspace experience stays focused on collaboration rather than administration.

At least one owner must remain in every workspace.
