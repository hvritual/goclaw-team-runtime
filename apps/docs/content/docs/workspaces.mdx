title: Workspaces
description: Create an isolated collaboration space for members, projects, issues, tasks, and skills.
---

A workspace is the top-level collaboration boundary in Multica. Its members, projects, issues, tasks, and skills are isolated from every other workspace.

## Create and switch

Create a workspace from the workspace picker, then give it a name and issue prefix. The prefix is used for readable issue identifiers such as `ACME-42`.

Use the workspace picker to switch contexts. Lists, searches, and settings always follow the workspace shown in the URL.

## Workspace settings

Owners and administrators can update the workspace name, description, icon, and issue prefix from **Settings → Workspace**. Member and role configuration sits beside it under **Settings → Members** and **Settings → Roles**.

Only owners can delete a workspace. Deletion removes its domain data and cannot be undone.
